### B. Trust Indicators

#### 1. Positive Trust Signals

- [ ] Keeps confidences - hasn't shared private information
- [ ] Shows up when they say they will
- [ ] Honest even when it's uncomfortable
- [ ] Celebrates your successes genuinely
- [ ] Available during difficult times
- [ ] Respects boundaries without needing reminders
- [ ] Mutual friends speak well of them

#### 2. Caution Signals

- [ ] **Gossip About Others:** Frequently shares others' private info (may do the same with yours)
- [ ] **Inconsistent Reliability:** Pattern of flaking or canceling
- [ ] **One-Sided Conversations:** Only talks about themselves, shows little interest in you
- [ ] **Competitive Energy:** Seems threatened by your success
- [ ] **Boundary Testing:** Pushes past stated limits or comfort zones
- [ ] **Fair-Weather Presence:** Absent during difficulties, present for fun
- [ ] **Uses Friendship as Leverage:** Brings up past favors or creates obligation

#### 3. Warning Signs

- [ ] **Manipulative Behavior:** Guilt-tripping, playing victim, emotional manipulation
- [ ] **Consistent Dishonesty:** Catches in lies or significant exaggerations
- [ ] **Negative Influence:** Encourages poor decisions or behavior
- [ ] **Information Gathering:** Asks invasive questions, seems to collect information
- [ ] **Social Undermining:** Speaks negatively about you to others
- [ ] **Financial Entanglement:** Repeatedly asks for money or favors
- [ ] **Isolation Attempts:** Discourages other friendships or relationships
