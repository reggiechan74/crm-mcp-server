### I. Behavioral Warning Signs Checklist

> **PATTERN RECOGNITION:** Track behaviors that indicate potential problems.

#### 1. Communication Patterns

- [ ] **Inconsistent Stories:** Different explanations to different people
- [ ] **Selective Transparency:** Open about some things, secretive about others
- [ ] **Blame Shifting:** Problems always caused by others, never them
- [ ] **Revisionist History:** Changing past narrative to suit current needs
- [ ] **Triangulation:** Using third parties to manipulate or gather information

#### 2. Negotiation Behaviors

- [ ] **Moving Goalposts:** Terms change after apparent agreement
- [ ] **Nickel and Diming:** Reducing value after commitment
- [ ] **Take-It-or-Leave-It Ultimatums:** Refusing to negotiate
- [ ] **Strategic Delays:** Using time to create pressure
- [ ] **False Scarcity:** Artificial urgency or competition claims

#### 3. Relationship Dynamics

- [ ] **Flattery Followed by Criticism:** Love-bombing then devaluation
- [ ] **Isolation Attempts:** Discouraging other relationships/opportunities
- [ ] **Obligation Creation:** Favors designed to create indebtedness
- [ ] **Gaslighting:** Making you doubt your own perceptions
- [ ] **Victim Positioning:** Always the wronged party

#### 4. Business Ethics

- [ ] **NDA Before Scope:** Requiring commitment before disclosure
- [ ] **Asymmetric Terms:** Different deals for similar contributions
- [ ] **History of Disputes:** Pattern of conflicts with partners/employees
- [ ] **Prolonged Unfilled Roles:** Market rejecting their terms
- [ ] **Vague Future Promises:** Equity/partnership "later" without specifics
